## Summary

The three Constitution principles actually invoked by name across the
real 011-018 Constitution Check tables: III (Filesystem Is the Single
Source of Truth — every disposable index/cache must be rebuildable
from the filesystem with zero data loss), IV (Simplicity First — YAGNI
& Minimal Configuration — no new package, command, or config field
without demonstrated need), and IX (Transparent, Machine-Readable
Contracts — internal commands return structured JSON, never
decorative output).
